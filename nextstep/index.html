Blind Spots

Everyone has them. Nobody can see their own. You can't fix what you can't see from where you sit, so this week, invite God and one other person to help you see.

Name your strand

Who's one person who could help you see what you can't? Same gender, someone you trust, someone who will be honest with you. Ask them before you leave the parking lot.
